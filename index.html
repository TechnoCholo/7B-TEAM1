The Skeletal System

Rigid framework of the human body with 206 bones, cartilage, ligaments, and tendons.

Functions include support, protection, movement, blood cell production, and mineral storage.

Types of Bones

Flat Bones

Protect internal organs and provide broad surfaces for muscle attachment.

Long Bones

Support body weight, allow movement, and store minerals.

Short Bones

Cube-shaped, providing stability and controlled movement.

Diseases & Injuries

Osteoporosis

Bones become porous and fragile due to decreased bone mass.

Osteoarthritis

Breakdown of cartilage causing bone-on-bone friction and pain.

Fractures

Cracks or breaks in bones, classified by type and severity.

Dislocations

Bones forced out of normal alignment at joints.

Parts of the Bone

Red Bone Marrow

Produces blood cells through hematopoiesis.

Yellow Bone Marrow

Stores fat for energy.

Compact Bone

Dense outer layer providing strength and protection.

Spongy Bone

Lightweight, porous structure reducing bone weight.

Fun Facts

The hyoid bone is not connected to any other bone.
The adult skeleton is replaced about every 10 years.
The smallest bone is the stapes (3 mm).
The "funny bone" is not actually a bone.
